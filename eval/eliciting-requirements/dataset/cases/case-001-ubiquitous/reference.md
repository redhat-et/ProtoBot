# Reference properties

- Classify the retention and retrieval obligations as ubiquitous unless a
  newly identified condition changes their meaning.
- Preserve the 30-day duration and the authorized-auditor boundary.
- Split independently verifiable retention and retrieval behavior or explain
  why one candidate is appropriate.
- Include observable evidence and pass/fail criteria for both behaviors.
- Define when the 30-day retention clock starts, how the exact expiry boundary
  is handled, and what evidence proves retention and expiry.
- Do not choose a database, table, cache, or storage service.
- Mark the result ready only if the authority definition and retrieval oracle
  are sufficiently defined; otherwise ask a focused question.
